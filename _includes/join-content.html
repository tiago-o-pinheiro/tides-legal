What it is

A quiet place to notice how you feel.

Once a day you note your mood and a single sentence. Tides quietly remembers the sky around it — the moon phase, the weather — so that, looking back, you can see how your inner weather moved. No streaks, no scores, no noise. It is a reflective space, not a productivity app.

How it works

Three screens, at your own pace.

Today

One check-in: a mood and a sentence. The moon phase and weather come along on their own.

Journey

Look back over past days. Anniversaries gently resurface — a week, a month, a year ago.

Notice

A short, calm reading of how the sky frames the day — written to be felt, not optimized.

Why your help

I built this alone, and I can't finish it alone.

To open Tides on the Play Store, Google asks that at least a dozen people test it for 14 days in a row. Beyond that rule, I genuinely want to know what feels off, what feels right, and where the words don't land. Your two weeks decide what ships.
