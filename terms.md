## 2. What Tides is, and what it is not

Tides is a **reflective journal** for noticing how you feel and how the
sky around you (weather, moon phase, astrological context) sits under
those feelings. You write a short reflection; Tides keeps it; on quiet
days, Tides offers a softly-phrased insight as one possible lens.

**Tides is not:**

- A medical service. We do not provide medical advice, diagnosis, or
  treatment. We do not assess mental-health conditions. If you are in
  crisis, please contact your local emergency service or a qualified
  professional.
- A therapy service. We are not a substitute for a therapist, a
  psychologist, a psychiatrist, or any qualified mental-health
  professional.
- A coaching, productivity, or self-improvement service. Tides has no
  goals, no streaks, no targets, no scores.
- A predictive service. We do not tell you what will happen tomorrow.
- A social network. Tides is solo. There is no way for another user to
  see what you wrote.

If anything inside Tides ever reads as though it is telling you what to
do, or what is going to happen, treat that as a writing failure on our
part — not as advice. Write to `tiopioli89@gmail.com` and we will fix
the wording.

## 4. The astrology disclaimer

Tides offers astrology as a **reflective frame**, not as a **predictive
system** and not as a **substitute for professional advice**. This
matters enough that it is its own section.

What this means in practice:

- An insight is an _invitation to notice_ — never a statement about
  what is going to happen, never a diagnosis, never a recommendation
  to act or not act. The voice we have written for is observational
  ("the Sun is brushing your Moon today") and open-ended (an open
  question, never a prescription).
- An insight is not a substitute for medical, mental-health, financial,
  legal, or relational advice. If a passage in Tides ever seems to be
  giving you advice that affects a real-world decision, that is the
  writing failing — the product does not intend that, and you should
  not rely on it.
- Astrology is offered with care. We treat the craft with the respect
  it deserves and we resist reducing it to either a punchline or a
  prophecy. This is a design choice, not a metaphysical claim. People
  who do not relate to astrology can use Tides as a plain mood journal
  by leaving the birth-data fields empty; the astrological surface
  fades to a minimum when you do.
- Weather information shown in Tides is sourced from third parties
  (currently Open-Meteo). It is provided for context, not for safety
  decisions. Do not use Tides for forecasting weather you depend on.

In short: Tides is a quiet room you bring a feeling into. It is not a
voice telling you what to do.
