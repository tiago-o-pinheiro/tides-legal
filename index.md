# Tides — Legal

The legal documents for the **Tides** mobile application — a private
mood-and-sky journal — published here as plain-text policies you can
read end-to-end without dark patterns or fine print games.

- [**Privacy Policy**](./privacy.html) — what we collect, why, the
  lawful basis under GDPR, who else processes your data, and how you
  exercise your rights.
- [**Terms of Service**](./terms.html) — what Tides is, what it isn't,
  the astrology disclaimer, acceptable use, and the limits we draw on
  liability and content licensing.

Both documents are working drafts pending review by a qualified lawyer
in Spain before public publication. Where any text here conflicts with
the Tides codebase, the codebase is the source of truth and the policy
must be updated, not the code.
